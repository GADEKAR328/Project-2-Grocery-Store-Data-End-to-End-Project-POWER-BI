# 📊 Swiggy Instamart Retail Analytics Dashboard (Power BI)

This project showcases a comprehensive retail analytics dashboard developed using Power BI to analyze the performance of a grocery delivery platform similar to Swiggy Instamart.

The dashboard transforms raw transactional and operational data into meaningful insights that help stakeholders understand business performance, customer behavior, and growth opportunities.

It is designed with a clean UI, interactive filters, and business-focused KPIs, making it suitable for both technical and non-technical users.

## 🎯 Project Objective

The primary objective of this project is to build an end-to-end business intelligence solution that can:

- Monitor overall sales and order performance  
- Evaluate store performance across different city tiers  
- Identify high-performing and low-performing product categories  
- Track customer satisfaction using ratings  
- Analyze store expansion trends over time  
- Enable data-driven decision-making for business growth  

---

## 📁 Dataset Overview

The dataset used in this project consists of structured transactional data including:

- 🧾 Order-level information (order count, sales value)  
- 🏪 Shop details (shop type, shop size)  
- 🌍 City classification (Tier 1, Tier 2, Tier 3)  
- 📦 Product categories (Fruits, Snacks, Dairy, Household, etc.)  
- ⭐ Customer ratings  
- 📅 Year-wise data (2021–2024)  

The data was cleaned, transformed, and modeled using Power Query and Data Modeling techniques.

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies Used

- Power BI → Dashboard development & visualization  
- Power Query → Data cleaning & transformation  
- DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) → Calculated measures & KPIs  
- Data Modeling → Relationship building for optimized performance

## 📊 Dashboard Overview

The dashboard is divided into multiple sections to provide a holistic view of business performance.

---

### 🔹 1. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The top section highlights core business metrics:

- 💰 Total Sales: 1.20M  
- 🛒 Total Orders: 9K  
- 📈 Average Sales: 141.01  
- ⭐ Average Rating: 3.92  

📌 Insight:  
These KPIs give a quick understanding of the business scale, efficiency, and customer satisfaction level.  
A consistent average rating (~3.9) indicates stable service quality across operations.

---

### 📅 2. Shops Opening Trend (Year-wise Analysis)

- 2021 → 2182 shops  
- 2022 → 2150 shops  
- 2023 → 2153 shops  
- 2024 → 2038 shops  

📌 Insights:

- Slight decline observed in 2024  
- Growth plateau between 2022–2023  

Possible reasons:
- Market saturation  
- Expansion strategy change  
- Increased competition  

👉 This metric is critical for understanding business expansion strategy and scalability.

---

### 🏙️ 3. Sales Performance by City Tier

- Tier 3 → 472K (Highest contribution)  
- Tier 2 → 393K  
- Tier 1 → 336K (Lowest)  

📌 Insights:

- Tier 3 cities generate the highest revenue  
- Indicates strong demand in semi-urban and developing regions  
- Tier 1 cities show lower contribution → possible saturation  

👉 Businesses can shift focus toward Tier 2 & Tier 3 expansion for higher growth.

---

### 🛍️ 4. Orders Distribution by City Tier

- Tier 3 → ~3K orders  
- Tier 2 → ~3K orders  
- Tier 1 → ~2K orders  

📌 Insights:

- Orders are fairly distributed across regions  
- Slight dominance from Tier 2 & Tier 3  
- Suggests a broad and diversified customer base  

---

### 🍽️ 5. Category-wise Sales Analysis

**Top Performing Categories:**

- Fruits & Vegetables → 0.18M  
- Snack Foods → 0.18M  
- Household → 0.14M  

**Low Performing Categories:**

- Meat → 0.06M  
- Soft Drinks → 0.06M  
- Health & Hygiene → 0.07M  

📌 Insights:

- Essential items dominate → daily usage drives sales  
- Snacks also perform strongly → impulse buying behavior  
- Low-performing categories need attention  

👉 Businesses should:

- Focus inventory on high-demand categories  
- Use promotions to boost low-performing segments  

---

### 🏪 6. Shop Type Performance Analysis

| Shop Type           | Total Sales | Avg Sales | Orders | Rating |
|--------------------|------------|----------|--------|--------|
| Grocery Store      | 1.51L      | 140.29   | 1083   | 3.93   |
| Supermarket Type1  | 7.87L      | 141.24   | 5577   | 3.92   |
| Supermarket Type2  | 1.31L      | 141.69   | 928    | 3.93   |
| Supermarket Type3  | 1.30L      | 139.80   | 935    | 3.91   |

📌 Insights:

- Supermarket Type1 dominates in both sales and orders  
- All shop types have similar average ratings  
- Operational consistency is maintained across formats  

👉 Suggests that scaling Supermarket Type1 could maximize revenue.

---

### 📏 7. Shop Size vs Customer Rating

- Small → ~3.92  
- Medium → ~3.93  
- High → ~3.90  

📌 Insights:

- Minimal variation in ratings across shop sizes  
- Customer satisfaction is independent of store size  

👉 Indicates strong standardization in service quality

## 💡 Key Business Insights

- Tier 3 cities are the top revenue contributors  
- Supermarket Type1 drives the majority of business  
- Fruits, Vegetables & Snacks dominate sales  
- Customer ratings are stable (~3.9 across all segments)  
- Decline in store openings in 2024 needs investigation  

---

## 🚀 Business Recommendations

- Expand operations in Tier 2 & Tier 3 cities  
- Increase stock & marketing for top-performing categories  
- Improve performance of low-demand categories  
- Analyze causes behind declining store openings  
- Maintain consistent service quality to sustain ratings
